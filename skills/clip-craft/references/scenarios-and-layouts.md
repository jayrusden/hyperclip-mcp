# Scenarios and layouts in detail

Timing numbers below are defaults. Prefer any number a tool returns, and anything the user states.

## Conversation
- Vertical or square canvas: one face fills the frame at a time. The talker by default. On a real reaction (a visible nod, laugh or turn at a payoff, confirmed in a frame, never invented) cut briefly to the listener, about a second or two, then back on a pause. A graphic goes beside the talker (`split`, or `split-stack` on tall canvases) and the listener is off screen for that span.
- Landscape: both speakers keep their own footage slot for the whole clip (`split`). A graphic is a third slot (`thirds`), it never takes a speaker's slot. A turn is a punch on the talker's slot.
- One footage slot squeezing two speakers is wrong on every canvas, unless the user asked to keep the footage as shot.

## Reveal
Details change more slowly toward the payoff. The payoff is the last change and it is held. If the stated length runs on past the payoff, the extra stretch is its own beat: it changes picture and each spoken result gets its own card.

## Points
Each line gets its own cut, layout change or card. A comparison may take one full-frame compare card as polish.

## Lesson
One speaker. Do not invent a second face. If the source repeats an analogy, keep the repetition. A heading already in the footage can stay with the speaker as a small inset.

## Optional polish (only when the beat earns it)
- a closing call-to-action card, only if the user asked for one or the source ends on one
- a graphic that points at a detail already visible in the footage

## Layout notes
- `full`: one thing fills the frame.
- `split`: the speaker stays the point; visual beside them.
- `pip`: visual is the point; corner is `top-left`, `top-right`, `bottom-left` or `bottom-right`.
- `thirds`: two speakers plus a visual.
- `show_at` / `hide_at` scope a layout to part of the clip; times are seconds on the clip timeline, not source time.
- Showing how something was made: the work and its result share one layout.
