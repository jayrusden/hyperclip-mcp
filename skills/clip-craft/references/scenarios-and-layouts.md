# Scenarios and layouts in detail

Timing numbers below are defaults. Prefer any number a tool returns, and anything the user states. Only captions and footage layouts exist in the beta: there are no graphics or cards.

## Conversation
- Vertical or square canvas: one face fills the frame at a time. The talker by default. On a real reaction (a visible nod, laugh or turn at a payoff, confirmed in a frame, never invented) cut briefly to the listener, about a second or two, then back on a pause.
- Landscape: both speakers keep their own footage slot for the whole clip (`split`). A turn is a punch on the talker's slot.
- One footage slot squeezing two speakers is wrong on every canvas, unless the user asked to keep the footage as shot.

## Reveal
Details change more slowly toward the payoff. The payoff is the last change and it is held. If the stated length runs on past the payoff, the extra stretch is its own beat: it changes picture.

## Points
Each line gets its own cut, punch or layout change.

## Lesson
One speaker. Do not invent a second face. If the source repeats an analogy, keep the repetition.

## Layout notes
- `full`: one picture fills the frame.
- `split`: two footage slots, side by side on landscape, stacked on tall canvases. `split-stack` stacks them.
- Every slot is `"footage"`. Other layouts (picture-in-picture, a visual beside the speaker) are not in the beta.
- `show_at` / `hide_at` scope a layout to part of the clip; times are seconds on the clip timeline, not source time.
